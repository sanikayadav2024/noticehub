About Us

Notice Hub is your centralized platform for streamlined, department-wise communication. We’re here to ensure that students stay updated with every notice, at the right time, from the right source.

Our Mission

To bridge the communication gap within academic institutions through a unified notice dissemination system.

Key Features

  • Organized Notices: Browse notices by department with ease.
  • User-Friendly Design: Simple, clean UI for quick navigation.
  • Search Functionality: Find specific notices instantly.
  • Real-Time Notifications: Get notified as soon as something’s posted.
  • Feedback Channel: Have your say—help us improve!

Our Vision

We aim to foster a connected, informed campus by making communication fast, reliable, and accessible for all.
